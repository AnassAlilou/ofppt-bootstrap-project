Must-Visit Places

Explore Morocco's most captivating destinations and create unforgettable memories

Fes

The Ancient Medina

Step into the world's largest car-free urban area and get lost in the labyrinthine alleys of Fes el-Bali. Discover ancient tanneries, traditional crafts, and a living medieval city that has barely changed in centuries.

  • World's largest medina
  • Famous leather tanneries
  • 9000+ narrow streets
Learn More

Marrakech

Jemaa el-Fnaa Square

Experience the heart of Morocco in the legendary Jemaa el-Fnaa square. By day, browse spice stalls and watch snake charmers. By night, feast at open-air food stalls while musicians and storytellers weave their magic.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Vibrant night markets
  • Historic souks nearby
Learn More

Essaouira

Wind City of Africa

Experience the laid-back coastal charm of this historic port city. Famous for windsurfing, fresh seafood, and a vibrant arts scene within ancient fortified walls.

  • UNESCO medina
  • World-class windsurfing
  • Fresh seafood markets
Learn More

Casablanca

Hassan II Mosque

Marvel at one of the world's largest mosques, perched dramatically on the Atlantic coast. The stunning Hassan II Mosque features intricate craftsmanship, a retractable roof, and a minaret reaching 210 meters into the sky.

  • Largest mosque in Africa
  • Ocean-front location
  • Guided tours available
Learn More

Chefchaouen

The Blue Pearl

Wander through the enchanting blue-washed streets of this magical mountain town. Every corner reveals stunning shades of blue, creating one of Morocco's most photogenic destinations.

  • Iconic blue architecture
  • Rif Mountains backdrop
  • Artisan handicrafts
Learn More

Tangier

Gateway to Africa

Where Europe meets Africa at the Strait of Gibraltar. This cosmopolitan city has inspired artists and writers for centuries with its unique blend of cultures and stunning views of Spain.

  • Caves of Hercules
  • Historic Kasbah
  • Cap Spartel lighthouse
Learn More

Ouarzazate

Hollywood of Morocco

Discover the gateway to the Sahara and Morocco's film industry capital. Home to Ait Benhaddou and Atlas Studios where Game of Thrones and Gladiator were filmed.

  • Ait Benhaddou UNESCO site
  • Atlas Film Studios
  • Desert excursions
Learn More

Merzouga

Heart of the Sahara

Experience the magic of Erg Chebbi, Morocco's most spectacular sand dunes. Ride camels at sunset and sleep under a blanket of stars in a luxury desert camp.

  • 150m high sand dunes
  • Camel trekking
  • Luxury desert camps
Learn More

Rabat

The Imperial Capital

Explore Morocco's elegant capital city with its blend of historic monuments and modern boulevards. Visit the iconic Hassan Tower and the picturesque Kasbah of the Udayas.

  • Hassan Tower landmark
  • Royal Palace
  • Kasbah of the Udayas
Learn More
